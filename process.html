How it works

From a photo on your phone to a mug on the counter.

You design it on this site. We send a picture of the mug before anything is printed. The picture is dyed into the ceramic. It is not a sticker.

The order

What you actually do

The short version is on the design page. This is the same path, with the details filled in.

1

Design it on the page

Start from a family photo, a kid's drawing, a dog, a few words in handwriting, or a funny line. Or use your own photo. Or tell us to design it. A funny line can be one we already like, or one you type.

2

Crop the photo, if it is yours

Drag the crop box. Drag a corner to resize it. Use the slider to zoom. Rectangle or circle. The page does not cut the background out. If you want that, say so in the note and the shop's designer does it.

3

Pick the side

One side is the usual. Right-hand side means the picture faces you when you hold the mug in your right hand. Left-hand side is the same idea for your left hand. Both sides is the same picture twice, and the price line shows the extra.

4

Put it on the order

Choose how many. The order page shows how close you are to free shipping. A gift can ship to someone else. Anonymous means we leave your name off, and we leave the prices off the packing slip. A short gift note is optional.

5

Say yes to the proof

Within one business day you get a picture of the mug. Want it bigger, moved, or a different photo? Say so. Nothing is printed until you say yes. Then it is usually five to seven days to the address on the order.

6

It is dyed in, not stuck on

The design is sublimated into the coating of the mug. There is no vinyl edge to catch with a fingernail, and nothing to peel in the dishwasher. Microwave safe too.

Design your mug

Dyed into the mug

Not a sticker. Not a decal.

A lot of custom mugs are a vinyl decal or a heat-transfer sheet pressed onto the outside. You can catch the edge of that print with a fingernail. Hot water works under the edge, and the picture peels.

We do it the other way. Dye sublimation on polymer-coated ceramic: under heat the ink turns to gas and settles inside the coating. When it cools, the picture is part of the surface. No edge to catch, nothing to lift.

Run your thumb across a finished one. You feel glaze, not a sticker.

The picture is on one side unless you chose both. The other side stays plain white. We proof it first, and we correct the color by hand so faces do not go orange.

Questions

What people ask before they order

Will a photo off my phone actually look good?
Almost always, yes. A phone photo from the last decade has plenty of detail for a mug. Screenshots, pictures squashed by a group text, and old scans are the risky ones. Send it anyway. A person looks at every file and tells you if it will not hold up, before it is printed.
How do I get my kid's drawing on there?
Lay it flat in daylight, hold the phone straight above it, and take one picture. Crop it on the design page. We straighten it and keep the crayon lines where your kid put them.
Does it survive the dishwasher?
Yes. The image is cured into the coating, so there is no edge for water to work under. Dishwasher and microwave are both fine. It will not peel.
How does my photo get to you?
Crop it on the design page. The crop, the words, the side, and any gift notes stay with the order. You do not email the photo. If the shop has a file field turned on at checkout, attach the same photo there too.
Can you take the background out?
The page will not. It only crops a rectangle or a circle. If you want the background removed, a stranger's elbow gone, or two photos put together, say so in the note. A designer at the shop does that. You can also ask on the proof.
Which side of the mug is the picture on?
One side, unless you choose both. Right-hand side faces you when you hold the mug in your right hand. Left-hand side faces you when you hold it in your left hand. The other side stays plain. Both sides uses the same picture twice, and the price on the order page includes the extra.
Can I send it as an anonymous gift?
Yes. In your order, mark it as a gift and put their address. Anonymous means we leave your name off and we leave the prices off the packing slip. A short note is optional. Checkout still asks you to confirm the address. We pack it from those notes.
Is shipping free?
The order page shows the free-shipping line, and how many mugs you are away from it. Under that amount, shipping is calculated at checkout. A big order for a wedding, a team, or a classroom is quoted separately. Write to us.
What if I do not like the proof?
Then we change it. Crop, words, which side, a different photo. Nothing is printed until you say yes.
I need these by a specific date.
Put the date in the note on the order and we will tell you honestly whether we can hit it. Ask before you count on a date.
Do you make anything besides mugs?
Mugs are the whole shop.
