Example pictures shipped with the storefront. They are samples of what a print can look like, not pictures of customers. Nothing on the page names the photographers.

Family, the recipe, the crew, and the two propped snapshots are under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). They are real photographs, stored in the repo so the page does not hotlink them. Kid's art is a drawing made for this example.

The mug shows a cutout: the `.png` file. People and the dog are the subject only, with the photo background removed. The recipe stays a page, with a clean edge and the table removed. Kid's art is the drawing with the paper cleared, so the crayon sits on the white mug. The `.jpg` beside a cutout is the picture that cutout was made from. Handwriting is typed on the page, not a photograph. A buyer's upload is not cut out on this page.
